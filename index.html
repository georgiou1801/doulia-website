Questions

Good to know

Is Doulia free?

Downloading Doulia and posting jobs is free. If a helper membership applies, the app always shows the price clearly before you pay anything, and your first job as a helper is free.

How do payments work?

You pay your helper directly, as agreed (cash or transfer). Doulia keeps track: the client taps "Yes, I paid", the helper confirms the money arrived, and the job is closed.

Can I suggest a different price?

Yes. Helpers can suggest a price when they apply, and the client can accept it, make a counter offer or decline.

Who can use Doulia?

Anyone aged 18 or over in the Republic of Cyprus, with one account per person.

What if something goes wrong?

Use "Report a problem" on the job. Our team reviews it and may contact you both. You can also block and report anyone at any time.
